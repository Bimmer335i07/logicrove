How to Get Your Law Firm Cited by AI Search: A 2026 Checklist

By LogicRove. Last updated July 2026.

When a prospect asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Google AI Overviews for a personal injury lawyer in your city, one firm gets named and the rest get skipped. Being that firm is not luck. It is the result of a handful of signals you can build on purpose. This is the checklist we use to do it.

Most firms treat AI visibility as a mystery. It is not. AI assistants recommend firms the same way a careful person would: they look for who is clearly real, clearly expert, clearly local, and clearly confirmed by other sources. Each item below strengthens one of those signals. Work through them in order and you move from invisible to recommended.

Start With the Question Your Client Actually Asks

Before you fix anything, write down the exact phrases a hurt person types when they need you. Not your practice name. The plain question. Things like "best car accident lawyer near me," "who should I call after a truck accident in my city," or "top personal injury attorney" with your metro attached. These prompts are the queries the assistants answer, so they are the queries you optimize toward. Everything that follows is about making your firm the confident answer to those specific questions.

Step One: Fix Your Structured Data

Schema markup is how a machine reads your site without guessing. Add LegalService, Attorney, and Review schema across your pages so an AI system can state plainly what you do, where you operate, and who leads the firm. Missing or broken schema is the single most common reason a firm never appears in a recommendation. For most sites this is a few hours of developer time and the effect lasts.

Step Two: Make Your Expertise Legible

AI models weigh experience, expertise, authority, and trust, the signals often shortened to E-E-A-T. Put real attorney names on every relevant page, with credentials, bar admissions, years in practice, and notable results where you can state them cleanly. A page authored by a named, credentialed attorney carries far more weight than anonymous marketing copy. The model wants to know who is speaking and why they are qualified. Tell it directly.

Step Three: Keep Your Firm's Facts Identical Everywhere

Entity consistency is quiet but decisive. Your firm name, address, phone number, and practice areas should read identically on your site, your Google Business Profile, every legal directory, and every review platform. When those facts disagree across the web, the model loses confidence that it knows which firm you are, and confidence is exactly what a recommendation requires. One afternoon spent aligning your listings removes a surprising amount of friction.

Step Four: Earn Third Party Confirmation

AI systems trust a firm more when independent sources agree it matters. A single directory profile is not enough. Build a consistent presence across the directories and review sites that carry weight in legal search, and pursue mentions in local publications, bar association pages, and reputable legal resources. Each independent source is a verification node the assistant can check against your own site. The firms that get named usually have several of these agreeing with each other.

Step Five: Write Content the Model Can Lift

Marketing copy persuades a human. Answer shaped content feeds a machine. Write pages that answer the real questions prospects ask, in plain declarative sentences a model can quote without editing. Tie your work to named cities and case types rather than vague phrases like "we serve the entire region." Specific, verifiable, geographically anchored writing is what gets pulled into an answer. Broad and abstract writing gets passed over.

Step Six: Measure First, Then Fix in Order

You cannot improve what you have not measured. Before you spend a dollar on remediation, find out where you actually stand: which assistants name you, which name your competitors, which sources those assistants cite, and which of the signals above you are weakest on. Then fix in order of impact rather than guessing. This is the difference between busywork and a plan.

What to Do First if You Only Have an Hour

If you can only do one thing today, audit your firm's facts. Open your website, your Google Business Profile, and your top three directory listings side by side, and make the name, address, phone, and practice areas match exactly. It is the cheapest fix on this list and it removes the doubt that keeps a model from saying your name. After that, add or repair your schema markup. Those two steps clear the most common blockers before you invest in content or citations.

Why This Order Matters

The steps compound. Clean facts make your schema believable. Believable schema makes your expertise legible. Legible expertise makes third party sources easier to earn. Earned sources make your content trustworthy enough to quote. Skip to writing content while your listings contradict each other and you build on sand. Work the list in order and each fix makes the next one land harder.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my law firm recommended by ChatGPT?
Strengthen the signals AI systems read: correct schema markup, clearly credentialed authorship, identical firm facts across the web, independent third party citations, and content that answers the exact questions prospects ask. Firms with these signals aligned are the ones ChatGPT names.
How long does it take to get cited by AI search?
The fastest wins, aligning your firm's facts and repairing schema, can register within weeks as sources are recrawled. Building third party citation coverage and answer shaped content is a longer effort that compounds over months. There is no instant switch, but there is a clear order of operations.
Do I need to fix every AI platform separately?
No. The signals overlap heavily. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews all reward consistent facts, credible authorship, structured data, and third party confirmation. Fixing those once improves your standing across every assistant at the same time.
Can my current marketing agency do this?
They can execute the remediation once you know what to fix. Most agencies do not yet diagnose AI visibility gaps, because the field is still new. A focused audit gives your team a prioritized list they can act on immediately.
How do I know which fix to do first?
